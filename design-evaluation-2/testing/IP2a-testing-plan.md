# IP2a Testing Plan

## Project
**Notion AR: Turning 2D Notes into 3D Spatial Ideas**

## What are we testing?

Can users understand and complete the core Notion AR flow on Meta Quest using the right controller, and do they find the 2D-to-3D idea useful?

### Core flow
**Open pages → Select House Sketch → Place on table → Reveal sketch → Turn into 3D → Move house**

## Test setup

- Meta Quest with IP2a installed
- Right controller
- Results sheet
- Timer
- Reset with **B** before each participant
- **3 participants** (recruited from friends who had already tried the earlier version of the prototype)
- About **5 minutes per participant**

> Note: this plan was first written for 5 participants. Testing was done with 3, so the plan and results table have been updated to match.

## What to tell the participant

> This is a Meta Quest prototype of a future Notion AR workspace. You will open a page, put it on the table, turn a 2D sketch into a 3D object, and move it. I am testing whether the interaction makes sense, not whether the app looks finished. Use the right controller and think aloud if anything feels confusing.

Do not explain the controls unless they are stuck for about 10 seconds.

## Test tasks

### 1. Open pages
Ask:
> Start when you're ready.

Watch whether they open the page picker.

### 2. Select and place
Ask:
> Select the House Sketch and put it on the table.

Watch whether they can select it and place it correctly.

### 3. Reveal the sketch
Ask:
> Show me what is on the page.

Watch whether they reveal the 2D sketch.

### 4. Turn it into 3D
Ask:
> Turn the sketch into a 3D object.

Watch whether they understand and complete the 2D-to-3D interaction.

### 5. Move the house
Ask:
> Move the house around the table.

Watch whether movement is understandable and comfortable.

## After the test

Ask:

1. What did you think this prototype was showing?
2. Was the table workspace clear?
3. Was the 2D-to-3D step clear and useful?
4. What was the most confusing part?
5. What would you improve?

Then record:

- **Ease:** 1–5
- **Usefulness:** 1–5

## What are we looking for?

After 3 participants, identify:

- Which steps caused the most confusion
- Whether people understood the 2D-to-3D idea
- Whether the table felt like a clear workspace
- Whether the concept seemed useful
- The main change to make for IP2b
